Tacos Recipe

Description: Delicious Mexican street food!

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 8 taco shells
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • Sour cream and salsa for serving

Steps

  1. Brown the ground beef in a skillet.
  2. Add the taco seasoning and water to the beef.
  3. Simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Warm the taco shells according to package instructions.
  5. Fill each shell with the beef mixture.
  6. Top with shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes, and shredded cheese.
  7. Serve with sour cream and salsa.
