Spaghetti Recipe

Description: Delicious Spaghetti!

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 packet spaghetti seasoning
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 8 spaghetti noodles
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • Sour cream and salsa for serving

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the spaghetti noodles according to package instructions.
  3. In a skillet, brown the ground beef and drain excess fat.
  4. Add spaghetti sauce to the beef and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. In a baking dish, layer noodles, meat sauce, and cheese. Repeat layers.
  6. Top with remaining cheese and cover with foil.
  7. Bake for 25 minutes, then remove foil and bake for an additional 10 minutes until cheese is bubbly.
  8. Let it cool for a few minutes before serving.
